Who is Lorena Cosentino?

Lorena Cosentino, Vice President of Empire Medical Training, has been instrumental in advancing aesthetic, anti-aging, and pain management education worldwide. She spearheads Empire's renowned training programs, workshops, and the signature Empire Aesthetics Meeting™, ensuring the highest standards in AMA-certified education.

With a deep knowledge of innovative treatments, skin care, and laser technology, Lorena has trained thousands of professionals, empowering them to elevate their practices. Her leadership extends to the Latin American market, where she played a pivotal role in launching Empire’s global expansion. She is Based in Ft. Lauderdale, FL, and continues to inspire through her work and philanthropic efforts.
